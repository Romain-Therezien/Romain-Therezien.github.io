Teaching assistant for the theory-oriented machine learning course at Telecom Paris (Winter 2025-2026 and Winter 2026-2027). Topics include Bayes-optimal prediction, statistical learning theory and regularization, linear classification (LDA, logistic regression), SVMs and kernels, bagging and boosting, and neural network theory. The course includes a hands-on project on the Avazu click-through-rate prediction challenge.

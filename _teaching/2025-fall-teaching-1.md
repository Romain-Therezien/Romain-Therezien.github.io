Teaching assistant for the intensive statistics course for first-year engineering students at Telecom Paris (Fall 2025 and Fall 2026). The course runs from probability foundations and limit theorems to confidence intervals, linear and logistic regression, maximum likelihood, and A/B testing. It ends with model selection, cross-validation, regularization and the bootstrap.

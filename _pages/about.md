Welcome to my academic homepage! I am a Ph.D. student at Institut Polytechnique de Paris - Telecom Paris in Applied Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Science under the supervision of [Stephan Clémençon](https://perso.telecom-paristech.fr/clemenco/), [Charlotte Laclau](https://laclauc.github.io) and [Pavlo Mozharovskyi](https://perso.telecom-paristech.fr/mozharovskyi/). 

# Research Topics
My research focuses on fairness in facial recognition, aiming to reduce bias and improve equity in facial recognition systems. I am particularly interested in developing and analyzing methods with strong statistical guarantees, such as uncertainty quantification and quantile regression, to better understand and improve their performance and reliability. My work lies at the intersection of statistics, machine learning, and computer vision.
